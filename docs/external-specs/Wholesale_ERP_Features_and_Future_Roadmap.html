📦 Executive Summary

The Vignesh GrowthLab Wholesale ERP is an enterprise-grade ERP designed specifically for wholesale stockists, distributors, multi-branch trading businesses, and logistics companies operating under Indian tax and statutory compliance laws. Built on a resilient FastAPI and MariaDB foundation with over 65 relational tables, it guarantees atomic row-level transaction safety, zero-drift inventory tracking, and full auditability.

🏛️ Complete Module Inventory (All 10 Core Modules)

1. Settings & System Master Configuration Module 1

  • Corporate Master: Company legal name, registered GSTIN, state code, PAN, CIN, and address.
  • Bank & UPI Profile: Company bank account details (IFSC, Account Number, Branch) and UPI ID.
  • Atomic Document Sequences: Row-locked sequence generator with financial year prefixes (e.g. GLB/26-27/001) preventing duplicate invoice numbers.
  • Dynamic Branding: Company logo and authorized signature upload rendered on all printable invoices and Delivery Challans.
  • Per-User Page Permissions: Granular feature access control allowing admins to customize sidebar visibility per employee.

2. Products & Inventory Management Module 2

  • HSN Validation: Strict validation of 4, 6, or 8 digit numeric HSN codes with default GST tax slab mapping.
  • 4-Tier Pricing Structure: Segregated price tiers: purchase_cost (procurement), b2b_price (wholesale), b2c_price (retail), and mrp.
  • Cost-Floor Guard: Enforces price hierarchy (Cost ≤ B2B ≤ B2C ≤ MRP) preventing below-cost invoicing errors.
  • Effective-Dated Scheduled Prices: Schedule price increases or decreases in advance; automatically activates when the effective date arrives.
  • True FIFO Stock Layer Valuation: Inward batches are tracked in stock_entries with individual purchase costs, depleted in chronological FIFO order during sales.
  • Below-Cost & Margin Alerts: Real-time in-app alerts alerting managers of low-margin sales or cost price spikes.
  • Bulk Operations: Mass CSV/Excel product catalog import and bulk price update tools.

3. Multi-Warehouse Management & Logistics Module 3

  • Multi-Location Tracking: Unlimited physical warehouses, regional hubs, and stockpoints.
  • Warehouse-Specific Banking: Configure local bank accounts or UPI IDs for regional branches on printouts.
  • Delivery Challan (DC) Transfers: Complete inter-warehouse transfer workflow: stock reservation at source → in-transit tracking → destination inspection & confirmation (or rejection & auto-restoration).
  • Physical Stock Adjustments: Audit tools for inventory count variance (Adjustment In adds FIFO batch; Adjustment Out consumes FIFO).
  • Stock Write-Off Workflow: Governance workflow for damaged or expired items requiring admin approval, auto-posting loss journals to the General Ledger.

4. Purchases & Vendor Management (Procure-to-Pay) Module 4

  • Vendor Master with GSTIN Auto-Fetch: Direct ClearTax API lookup auto-populates legal vendor business name and state code.
  • Purchase Bill Entry: Inward bill capture with duplicate invoice number detection and automatic Intra-State (CGST+SGST) vs Inter-State (IGST) tax calculation.
  • FIFO Layer Creation: Every purchase entry generates a tracked FIFO layer and updates product cost history.
  • Double-Entry Posting: Automatically debits Stock-in-Trade & Input Tax Credit (ITC) while crediting Sundry Creditors (Vendor).
  • FIFO Payment Allocation: Allocates payments across vendor bills oldest-first with advances tracking.
  • Protected Cancellation / Voids: Voiding a purchase reverses inventory, ledger, and tax credit (guarded against already-consumed stock).

5. Sales, Billing & Invoicing (Order-to-Cash) Module 5

  • Customer Master & Credit Limits: Customer profiles with credit ceilings, payment terms (days), and multiple delivery addresses.
  • Quotations & Estimates: Instant price quotes convertible into active B2B or B2C tax invoices with 1 click.
  • Tax Invoices (B2B & B2C): Real-time warehouse stock checks, credit limit validation, serial numbers, discounts, and round-off calculation.
  • FIFO COGS Capture: Freezes historical FIFO cost on each invoice item to compute exact gross profit margins.
  • Credit Notes (Sales Returns): Restores goods into inventory as return_in, reverses GST liability, and credits customer balance.
  • A4 Printable Invoices: Clean layout with GST breakup summary, declarations, bank details, and E. & O.E.
  • WhatsApp Invoice Sharing: Formatted WhatsApp text with Indian rupee formatting (₹12,34,567.89) and direct web links.

6. Financial Accounting & General Ledger Module 6

  • Standard Indian Chart of Accounts: Pre-configured heads for Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, and Expenses.
  • Automated Double-Entry Posting: Every commercial transaction auto-posts balanced debit/credit journal entries in real time.
  • Receivables Ageing Buckets: Breakdown into 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, and 90+ days with Collection Priority Scoring.
  • Daily Cash Closing: Reconciles opening cash + daily receipts − cash expenses with physical cash count verification.
  • Expense Approval Workflow: Expenses above a configurable threshold (e.g. ₹5,000) require admin sign-off before posting.
  • TDS & Form 26AS: Withholding tax tracking across sections 194C, 194J, and 194Q with Form 26AS reconciliation.
  • Live Trial Balance: On-demand balanced trial balance across any custom date range or financial year.

7. Statutory Taxation (GST, E-Invoice & E-Way Bill) Module 7

  • ClearTax Cloud Integration: Supports live government portal integration or deterministic sandbox mock mode.
  • E-Invoicing (IRN & QR): 1-click IRN generation and signed QR code persistence for B2B invoices with 24-hour cancellation rule.
  • E-Way Bill Logistics: Generates E-Way bills with vehicle master data, transporter IDs, and distance calculations.
  • GST Returns: GSTR-1 outward supplies breakdown, GSTR-2B inward reconciliation vs purchases, and GSTR-3B monthly tax summary.

8. Banking & Treasury Management Module 8

  • Cheque Register: Complete lifecycle (Received → Deposited → Cleared / Bounced). Bouncing auto-reverses ledger entries.
  • Bank Statement Reconciliation: Import bank statement CSV/Excel files and auto-match transactions against accounting ledger entries by date and amount.
  • Bank Stock Statement (Drawing Power): Valuates FIFO stock, filters eligible debtors (<90 days), and generates banker-certified Drawing Power (DP) certificates for Cash Credit (CC) limits.

9. Executive Reports & Analytics Module 9

  • Live Management Dashboard: Isolated resilient queries reporting Revenue, Receivables, Payables, Cash in Hand, and Stock Value.
  • P&L Statement: Gross Profit (Revenue − FIFO COGS) and Net Profit (deducting operating expenses).
  • Inventory Valuation: Side-by-side comparison of FIFO valuation vs replacement/latest purchase cost.
  • Day Book: Chronological register of all sales, purchases, receipts, and payments for any single day.

10. Security, RBAC & Audit Governance Module 10

  • Role-Based Access Control: 5 roles (super_admin, admin, accountant, sales, warehouse) with warehouse scoping.
  • Session & Security Controls: JWT access & refresh tokens, active device session manager, force logout, and brute-force lockout.
  • Audit Trail (ActivityLog): Immutable logs tracking user actions, IP addresses, and data changes for complete traceability.
  • DPDP Act Ready: Compliant data retention policies and user privacy erasure controls.

🚀 Future Expansion Roadmap

The Wholesale ERP architecture is designed with clean modular interfaces to support the following upcoming features:

1. Online Payment Gateway (Razorpay / Cashfree)

Instant SMS/WhatsApp payment links and webhook listeners that auto-create Customer Receipts and settle invoice balances in real time.

2. Dynamic NPCI UPI QR on Invoices

Printed dynamic UPI QR codes containing the invoice reference and exact payable amount for scan-and-pay via Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm.

3. WhatsApp Business Cloud API Bot

Automated invoice delivery, dispatch alerts, overdue payment reminders, and customer self-service ledger statements via WhatsApp.

4. Tally Prime / ERP 9 Two-Way Sync

Direct XML/ODBC export bridge to transfer sales, purchases, and journals into Tally Prime without manual re-entry for external auditors.

5. Mobile Barcode & RFID Warehouse Scanner

Handheld barcode scanner and mobile camera app for Goods Receipt (GRN), cycle counts, and zero-error dispatch order picking.

6. Temporal Regulatory Configuration Engine

Effective-dated tax rules and thresholds stored in the database so historical invoices always calculate with the exact statutory laws of that date.

7. AI Demand & Inventory Forecasting

Predictive consumption analysis evaluating sales velocity, seasonality, and vendor lead times to generate smart Reorder POs before stockouts occur.

8. B2B Customer & Vendor Self-Service Portals

Dedicated client portal where wholesale buyers can place orders, view invoices, download statements, and track dispatch shipments.
